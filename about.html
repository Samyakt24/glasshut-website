The Story of GlassHut

◆ ◆ ◆

Rooted in Firozabad

Born in the heart of India's Glass City, GlassHut is a proud continuation of a legacy that began in 1984 under New Milan Trader. For four decades, we have been dedicated to the art of crafting authentic, traditional chudiyan. What started as a local passion for brilliant colors and flawless glasswork has blossomed into a brand that delivers the vibrant spirit of Firozabad directly to your doorstep.

The Art of Craftsmanship

We believe that true beauty lies in the details. Every single bangle in our collection is meticulously handmade by expert artisans. Because we use traditional melting and shaping techniques, slight variations in color and thickness are not defects—they are the natural, beautiful marks of true craftsmanship. From timeless classics to trending aesthetic sets, our catalog features over 200 distinct color shades designed to perfectly match any occasion.

Our Promise to You

Your trust means everything to us. We are committed to providing not only the finest handcrafted jewelry but also a seamless shopping experience. We ensure safe transit with secure packaging so your beautiful pieces arrive pristine. At GlassHut, you aren't just purchasing accessories; you are taking home a piece of Indian heritage, crafted to shine.
